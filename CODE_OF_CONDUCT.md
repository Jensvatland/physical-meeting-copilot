# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our
community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness
- Being respectful of differing opinions and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Staying honest about project capabilities (simulated vs production paths)

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, trolling, or derogatory comments
- Publishing others’ private information without permission
- Sexualized language or imagery
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional or community setting

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns to the repository maintainers (GitHub repository owner
for [Jensvatland/physical-meeting-copilot](https://github.com/Jensvatland/physical-meeting-copilot)).
For security vulnerabilities, use [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead of a public issue.

Maintainers will review and respond as appropriate. They may remove or reject
contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.
